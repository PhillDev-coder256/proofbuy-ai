TITLE:
ProofBuy AI — Evidence-Backed AI Shopping with Verified PayPal Checkout

DESCRIPTION:

ProofBuy AI lets shoppers describe what they need in plain language and instantly
get evidence-backed product recommendations they can purchase securely with PayPal.

The full flow demonstrated:
1. Describe your need — "M3 MacBook Pro under $1500 with 16GB RAM"
2. AI extracts requirements — Groq parses brand, model, processor, RAM, price (fully editable)
3. Real product search — Channel3 searches 100M+ products with images
4. Constraint validation — Deterministic PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN per attribute (100% code, AI never evaluates constraints)
5. Ranked results with evidence-backed explanations
6. PayPal checkout — Server-side Orders v2 capture with verification

Also shown: Google Sign-In (Firebase), View All Products with price/rating sorting,
and All Categories browse.

Built with Flutter + Riverpod (mobile), FastAPI (backend), Groq AI, Channel3 API,
PayPal Orders v2, Firebase Auth, Render.

GitHub: https://github.com/PhillDev-coder256/proofbuy-ai
Live backend: https://proofbuy-ai-backend.onrender.com/health
